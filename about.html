Our story

Built for the Days Your Family Remembers

Al-Hamza Caterers began with a simple idea: neighbourhood events deserve the same care as grand weddings. Today we cater shaadis, walimas, engagements, birthdays and office gatherings across Okhla and South Delhi.

We keep things honest — no tall claims, no shortcuts. Fresh cooking, generous portions, neat presentation and a team that arrives on time and treats your guests with adab and warmth.

Meet the founder

Atiqur Rehman

Founder, Al-Hamza Caterers

From Jamia Nagar, Okhla, Atiqur Rehman leads Al-Hamza Caterers with a simple belief — every gathering, from a family daawat to a grand shaadi, deserves fresh food, honest flavours and warm hospitality.

He stays closely involved in menu planning, food presentation and on-day service, so guests are served with care and every buffet counter looks its best. For him, a successful event is one where the hosts can relax and the guests remember the meal fondly.
